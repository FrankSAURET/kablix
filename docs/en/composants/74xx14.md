# 74xx14 — hex Schmitt-trigger inverter

![74xx14](../../img/composants/74xx14.webp)

DIL-14 package for the breadboard: **six independent inverters**. An inverter outputs the **opposite** of its input — 1 becomes 0, 0 becomes 1.

74 series: the “xx” in the part number is its **family** (HC, LS, HCT…). It changes neither the function nor the pinout, but it sets the **supply range** — hence which board the chip works on.

| Family | Supply |
|---|---|
| HC, AC, ACT | 2 to 6 V |
| C | 3 to 15 V |
| HCT, ALS, F | 4.5 to 5 V |
| S, LS | 4.75 to 5.25 V |

An Uno puts out 5 V: every family works. A **Pico only puts out 3.3 V**: only HC, AC, ACT and C work there — a 74LS14 on a Pico does nothing.

These inverters are **Schmitt-trigger** ones: on real hardware their switching threshold rises on the way up and drops on the way down, which cleans up a slow or noisy signal. Levels are clean in simulation, so the hysteresis never shows. This is the 74-series twin of the [CD40106](cd40106.md).

| 6 | **c̅** | output of inverter C (bar = inverted) |

| 8 | **d̅** | output of inverter D (bar = inverted) |

## Truth table

One inverter; the other five are identical.

| a | a̅ |
|---|---|
| 0 | 1 |
| 1 | 0 |

## Properties

| Property | Role | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `ref` | Part number — changing it changes the pinout, not the drawing | 74xx14 |
| `family` | 74-series family: it decides the supply range | HC |

## Simulation

- **Power is not optional**: without a red wire on VCC (pin 14) and a black wire on GND (pin 7), the chip outputs nothing at all.
- Outside the range **of the chosen family (2 to 6 V for HC)** Kablix reports “Incompatible supply voltage”. **Below it** the chip does nothing; **above it** the chip is DESTROYED — red cross, and it stays that way until the next run.
- A **floating input is not a 0**: the output stays undefined as long as that input matters — and for this function it always matters.
- An output drives the circuit just like a board pin: an LED **with its resistor**, a transistor base, or the input of another chip.

## Usage

- An inverter turns a logic level into its opposite; two in a row leave the level alone but **clean the signal up**.
- The 6 inverters in the package are independent: one chip can serve 6 unrelated purposes.
- Wire the inputs to board outputs and the output to a board input: the program then reads a result computed by the chip, not by the microcontroller.
- Ready-made test: `CI-uno` and `CI-pico` in `testkablix/` — the twelve chips wired together, every gate checked.
